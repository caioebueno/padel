# Movement fluency

Why the players looked robotic after phase 2 of the movement realism plan, and what we are doing about it. The full analysis (two reference videos: a console tennis game used only as a reference for feel, and real pro padel) and the plan Caio approved on 2026-11-16 are in the project's "Fluid player movement plan" doc. After F1, a playtest found the motion smoother but still generic, not padel; a third video (a professional match from the broadcast camera) gave the "Padel motion catalogue and plan" doc: what real players do in every situation and every shot, and the revised order below (approved 2026-11-17). This file is the in-repo summary that code comments point to.

## The diagnosis

The movement simulation (`FPlayerMotion`, `Data/Movement.json`) is fine. The pose on top of it was stiff:

- Four looping walk and jog clips blended by direction on one phase: no starts, stops, plants or reversals.
- No swing clips: the arm reaches for the ball with IK, the body doesn't turn or shift weight.
- The body turned at a constant 540°/s, all in one piece.
- 0.08 s linear blends, so state changes pop.
- No lean, no pelvis motion, an invisible split step, a frozen ready stance.

## The rule: the simulation leads, the pose follows

Movement stays code-driven in `PadelCore` (deterministic, used by the AI and the soak gate). The animation adapts to it: springs, warping, foot locking and motion matching on the simulation's trajectory. Root motion is not used.

## Why it still looked generic after F1

Real players are recognisable from the stands for three reasons, none of which F1 gave us: a **stance for each spot and moment** (net, back, receiving, serving, between points), a **body shape for each shot** (every shot was the same arm reach: none of `AnimClips.json`'s 42 swing clips exists), and the **rhythm of the point** (split step on every opponent contact, eyes on the ball, side-on for lobs, the wait off the glass, the pair moving as one).

## The steps

| Step | What | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **F1 body dynamics** | Facing on a spring with the head and chest leading the hips; trunk lean into the acceleration; the pelvis sinking on brakes and landings with the feet planted; a visible split-step hop; a light ready-stance bounce. Model: `FPlayerAnimModel::StepBody`; pose: `PadelBodyDynamics`. Data: `Data/Animation.json` (`Facing*HalfLifeS`, `FacingMax*TwistDeg`, `Lean*`, `PlantDrop*`, `PelvisHalfLifeS`, `SplitHopHeightM`, `SplitLand*`, `ReadyBounce*`). | done |
| **P1a stances and the head on the ball** | A stance per spot and moment (`EStance`: Relaxed, Serve, Receive, Net, Back), each with its crouch, racket carry, feet width, knee bend and trunk tip; before the serve the server, receiver and partners each take theirs (`EServeRole`); in a rally Net up to `StanceNetUntilM` from the net, Back from `StanceBackFromM`, blended between; all on a spring. A swing rises out of the stance (its crouch, knee bend, trunk tip and width fade with the swing weight), so the reach starts from full height and the contact warp bends only as far as the ball needs. The head follows the ball (yaw within `HeadLookMaxYawDeg` of the chest, pitch within `HeadLookMin/MaxPitchDeg`). Model: `FPlayerAnimModel::StepStance`, `StepBody`; pose: `PadelBodyDynamics` (`FeetWidenUnits`, `HeadPitchDeg`). Data: `Data/Animation.json` `Stances`, `Stance*`, `HeadLook*` (replacing `ReadyCrouchAlpha*`, `ReadyRacket*`, `ArmReadyAlpha`). | done |
| **P1b side-on retreat** | Going back with a ball to watch (a lob over the net player), the body turns side-on by `BackRunTurnDeg`, racket shoulder back, and crosses over with the head on the ball, instead of turning its back on it; without a ball to watch (between points) it still turns all the way to run. Model: `StepLocomotion`. | this PR |
| P1b, rest | The wait off the glass for the human player too (the AI's glass read covers only AI players today; needs the AI area), the split-step landing toward the first step, the between-points walk and the ball bounce before the serve. | next |
| P2 shot bodies | Key poses per shot and phase (unit turn timed by time to contact, backswing, contact, finish, recovery steps) in `Data/ShotPoses.json`, so each shot has its own silhouette. Chosen by Caio: build them now, replace with captured padel later. | after P1 |
| P3 motion matching | Epic's Game Animation Sample locomotion database for starts, stops and pivots (legs and hips; the stance owns the upper body). | parallel (Mac) |
| **M1 motion matching** | P3's first pass, approved 2026-11-17 ("FIFA-level smoothness"). The C++ pose's 14-loop mix is replaced by motion matching over the Game Animation Sample's Dense Stand set: walk, run and sprint loops, starts, stops and pivots, the idles and the turns in place (447 clips, 14 databases), retargeted onto the placeholder skeleton with the sample's schemas (trajectory at −0.05 to 1.0 s; feet position and velocity, pelvis heading), one normalization set, no mirroring. `FPadelMotionMatcher` (`PadelMotionMatching.h`) owns its pose history, calls `UPoseSearchLibrary::MotionMatch` with the model's trajectory as an `FTransformTrajectory`, and crossfades its picks (`MotionMatchingBlendTimeS`, smooth ease; a new pick waits for `MotionMatchingRepickAfterBlend`, and a full stack drops the clip that shows least) at a play rate matching each clip's root speed to the simulation's (`MotionMatchingPlayRateMin`/`Max`). A chooser in data, like the sample's chooser table, picks which databases a search uses: idles when coming to rest, stops while still slowing after moving, turns in place after a turn, otherwise the walk, run or sprint starts, loops and pivots by speed (`MotionMatching{Still,Stop,TurnInPlace,Walk,Sprint}Databases`, `MotionMatchingStillBelowMps`, `StopAfterM`, `TurnInPlaceDeg`, `RunFromMps`, `SprintFromMps`, `GaitOverlap`). The trajectory's future carries on the player's acceleration (braking ends at rest), so stops and starts show before they happen. The simulation still leads (clips root locked); swings, the celebration, F1/P1 body dynamics and the arm reach run on top unchanged. Over mocap legs the stance's crouch, feet widen and pelvis drops fade with speed and the trunk lean lightens (`MocapLeg*`, `MocapLeanKeep`). The loop mix stays as the fallback (no databases, or before the first pick). Pipeline: `gasp_export.py` (clips and `motion_matching.json`), `animation_setup.py` with `UPadelMotionMatchingBuilder`. Data: `Data/Animation.json` `MotionMatching*`, `Mocap*`, `TrajectoryTimesS`. | this PR |
| **M2 firm motion** | After Caio's M1 playtest ("still a little floppy and not so human like"), measured first with a probe on M1's scripted path, then each fix kept only where it helped. (1) Fewer, cleaner switches: the search runs every `MotionMatchingSearchThrottleS` (0.1 s; at once when a clip that doesn't loop is running out), a candidate must beat the continuing pose's cost by `MotionMatchingSwitchCostMargin`, `MotionMatchingPoseJumpThresholdS` 0.5 s, and a switch is an inertialization (one clip plays; the difference from the pose shown, and its velocity, decay on a spring of `MotionMatchingInertialHalfLifeS`, 0.25 s) instead of up to four clips averaging in a crossfade. (2) The trunk lean over mocap legs down to `MocapLeanKeep` 0.1; fading the chest's facing lead (`MocapChestTwistKeep`) and capping the head (`MocapHeadMaxTwistDeg`) were built and measured, and made the head and spine jerkier, so they stay off (1.0, 180). (3) `FPadelLocomotionWarp` (`PadelLocomotionWarp.h`): orientation warping (hips toward the simulation's travel, at most `OrientationWarpMaxDeg`, spine turned back), stride warping (`StrideWarpMin`..`Max`) and foot locking (`FootLock*`: a foot the clip plants stays put in the world until the clip lifts it or it drifts `FootLockMaxDriftM`). (4) The MetaHuman retarget adds no lag: pelvis and head within 1° of the placeholder's, 0 frames behind (spine_03 bends up to ~30° differently, a chain distribution, with less jerk than the placeholder). Numbers in Checks. | this PR |
| M2, rest | A trajectory from the movement intent rather than the velocity carried on, facing predicted for the turn clips, and the shuffle and crossover sets (Relaxed / strafe databases) for padel's sideways footwork. The stops and the 180° reversal still skate (the simulation brakes and turns harder than any clip; the lock lets go when the leg would overstretch). | next |
| P4 real padel capture | Phone-based markerless capture of a club player using the catalogue as the take list, replacing P2's poses shot by shot; the studio session stays for later. | when footage exists |
| F1 inertial blending, foot locking | Kept from the first plan: inertialization for discrete switches after P1, foot locking with P3. | later |

## Checks

- Native (`Tests/Native/PlayerAnimBodyTests.cpp`, `Anim.Body*`): the springs settle on their targets with no jump in turning speed; the head and chest lead a turn within their caps; lean follows the acceleration's direction and is capped; braking and landing sink the pelvis; the split step peaks at `SplitHopHeightM`; the ready bounce only plays standing ready in a rally.
- Native (`Tests/Native/PlayerAnimStanceTests.cpp`, `Anim.Stance*`): every stance is listed once and they differ as real players stand (the receiver lowest and widest, the racket higher at the net, lower and wider at the back, relaxed racket down); the rally blends net to back with no jumps; the four serve roles take their stances; the head follows the ball up, down and behind within its caps, and the server's partner doesn't look back at the serve.
- Native (`Anim.Stance: running back for a lob…`): both hands turn side-on by `BackRunTurnDeg` with the racket shoulder back, travel sideways to the body, head up on the ball.
- Unreal (`Padel.Animation.MotionMatching`, M1 and M2, probe in `Private/Tests/PadelMotionProbe.*`): one pawn driven along stand, accelerate to a run, run 3 m, stop, 180° reversal, side shuffle, stop, at 60 Hz, twice: the clips alone, and the full stack (the animation model turning, leaning and looking at a ball; a shown MetaHuman measured too). M1 → M2, placeholder, clips alone: clip switches 1.96 → 1.52 a second; poses at once up to 4 → 1; pelvis jerk p95 219k → 226k deg/s³, spine_03 260k → 240k, head 118k → 145k; planted foot slide steady 18.6 → 21.2 cm/s, over the path 41.4 → 44.8 (the throttled search alone took steady to 28.7, the warps and locking back to 21.2); largest bone turn in a frame 18.0° → 18.8°. Full stack: switches 2.07 → 1.31, steady slide 33.6 → 30.1, MetaHuman foot slide 65 → 73 over the path but 41 → 51 steady (its ankles only; fewer, longer planted spells). Limits: under 1.8 switches a second, at most 2 poses, pelvis jerk p95 under 245k and spine_03 under 255k, steady slide under 25 (full stack 33), whole path under 50, no body bone over 20° a frame, the MetaHuman 0 frames behind. Skipped without the databases.
- Unreal (`Padel.Animation.BodyDynamics`): on the skeleton, a pelvis drop keeps the feet within 1 cm, the hop lifts them, a chest twist turns the shoulders, a forward lean moves the head toward the net, the stance sets the feet wider with both on the ground, and a head pitch lifts the face. `Padel.Animation.SwingClearance` now starts every swing from each stance's racket carry.
